Sources of https://kafka-docker-playground.io (Docsify, https://docsify.js.org, theme https://jhildenbiddle.github.io/docsify-themeable/#/).

Hand-written pages and templates are tracked here. scripts/publish-docs.sh publishes this folder, together with the pages
generated by 'playground update-docs' / 'playground update-readme' / update-changelogs.yml, to
https://github.com/vdesabou/kafka-docker-playground-docs, which serves the website and keeps the media
(videos/, images/, reports/) and the scripts recording them.

Preview locally (images and videos only resolve on the website):

$ npx docsify-cli serve ./docs
