🔘 clickhouseClientInsertTimeoutMs

Maximum time in milliseconds to wait for a single ClickHouse insert response. Must stay below consumer.override.max.poll.interval.ms: if an insert hangs longer than the poll interval, Kafka assumes the worker is dead and rebalances the partitions mid-insert. Leave a comfortable margin (e.g. at least 2x the slowest expected insert) so transient slow inserts do not race the poll interval. On timeout a RetriableException is thrown, so the Connect framework retries the batch; in exactly-once mode the retry reuses insert_deduplication_token and ClickHouse drops duplicates. Default: 240000 ms.

	 - Type: LONG
	 - Default: 240000
	 - Importance: LOW
	 - Required: false
